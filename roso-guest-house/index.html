02 / THE DISCOVERY GAP

People ask for
what Roso offers.

OBSERVEDRoso appeared for pet-friendly stays in ChatGPT and for terrace-plus-breakfast on Claude Places. It was missing from the broad boutique-weekend lists on all four scored engines.

Our reading

AI systems can identify the hotel when asked directly. The more useful opportunity is helping them select Roso for specific guest needs — design, neighborhood, breakfast, terrace and pet-friendly travel.

What this does not mean

These are single answers, not evidence that Roso has a fixed position or that a particular page change will move it by a set number of places.

The next benchmark should repeat the same questions over time and measure direct-booking visits alongside recommendation accuracy.

03 / FACTS THAT MATTER

Make every answer
easy to verify.

OBSERVEDClaude recognized Roso but did not give the DECU direct-booking page. ChatGPT's pet-friendly answer repeated a third-party “two pets” policy, while DECU's page says one pet per room.

What we would fix first

Confirm the live pet policy with your team. Then make the booking URL, policies, room types, breakfast and terrace details consistent across the official pages and the outside sources guests and AI tools use.

Small technical improvements

In the HTML we fetched, the Spanish property page had an English title and meta description. We would localize those and review structured hotel data with your web team. Its canonical and language-alternate links were already present.

The aim is clearer, more accurate recommendations — without replacing your existing marketing work.

Page-code observations do not prove why any AI answer did or did not include Roso. Structured data may also be injected after the HTML fetch; that was not audited.

04 / A FOCUSED GEO PROGRAMME

Give AI search better reasons
to choose Roso.

01 / MAKE THE FACTS SOLID

Foundation

A clear, consistent source of truth for every key guest question.

  • Confirm policies, amenities, location and the direct-booking path with the Roso team.
  • Improve the Spanish and English property copy, page titles and practical FAQs.
  • Review hotel structured data and priority third-party listings with the web team.

Deliverable: corrected pages and a short source-consistency checklist.

02 / BUILD REASONS TO RECOMMEND

Content

Original, useful material based on the experience Roso already offers.

  • Document the rooms, design, terrace, breakfast and neighborhood with real details and imagery.
  • Create a small set of guest-intent pages or articles, not generic AI-written volume.
  • Retest the same prompts and watch qualified direct-booking traffic and enquiries.

Deliverable: a focused content plan and a repeatable visibility report.

First confirm which information your current team already maintains. We would work alongside them, with their approval of operational facts.

05 / A SEPARATE PAID LEVER

Test ChatGPT Ads
with one clear offer.

Organic AI recommendations and paid placements are different channels. We would improve Roso's unpaid discoverability, then test whether a small, controlled ad campaign produces qualified visits or bookings.

01 / OFFER

Pick an occasion

For example, a Roma Norte weekend stay. The team chooses the offer, terms and landing page.

02 / TEST

Set a firm budget

Launch only if Roso's account is eligible and the team approves creative, spend and tracking.

03 / DECIDE

Measure bookings

Review qualified visits, enquiries and booking outcomes before expanding the budget.

The point is to learn early, with evidence —
not to buy a place in organic answers.

Ads cannot be assumed to improve organic rankings. Availability, formats and targeting depend on the current product and account eligibility. No cost-per-booking or ROI estimate is possible without campaign data. OpenAI ad information ↗
